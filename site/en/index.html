Your FLAC music, played exactly as it was recorded.

Toxify is a Windows player for people who keep their own library. It decodes FLAC and hi-res without losing a bit, shows lyrics with a translation under each line and builds mixes from what you listen to most.

Free and open source. No account and no administrator password.

Toxify is for Windows. Open this page on your computer to download and install it.

What it does

Lossless playback
The decoder is checked bit by bit against ffmpeg: the project's test compares 68 million samples of a 24-bit/96 kHz FLAC and every one matches. In exclusive mode the audio goes straight to the sound card, bypassing the Windows mixer, and the sample rate switches with each track.
Crossfade that respects the album
Gapless tracks, like on a live album, play back to back with no pause. Everywhere else the transition starts where the sound ends, not where the file ends, and skips the silence at the start of the next track.
Lyrics with translation
Lyrics synced to the music, with a translation under each line in the app's language. Also available in full screen.
Mixes that learn your taste
Mixes by genre, decade and artist, built from what you play to the end and what you skip. Everything is computed on your computer; nothing leaves it.
Tag editor
Fixes title, artist, album, year, track and genre right in the file. Each save goes to a copy, gets checked to make sure the audio is still identical and only then replaces the original. You can undo.
Library cleanup
Finds the same artist spelled two ways, site names stuck in titles and discs of the same album showing up apart, and shows what will change before applying it.
Windows and Discord
Works with your keyboard's media keys and the Windows media controls, and shows "Listening to Toxify" on your Discord profile. All of it can be turned off.

How to install

  1. Download the installer

    Toxify-Setup.exe is about 10 MB. Open it when the download finishes.

  2. Confirm in Windows

    The installer isn't code-signed yet. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway. Each release is built on GitHub from the public source, with an attestation you can verify.

  3. Open and listen

    Toxify looks for music in your Windows Music folder. If yours is somewhere else, pick the folder in Settings.

Open source

Toxify's source is on GitHub under the MIT license: read it, build it, change it. To uninstall, use Settings > Apps > Installed apps, like any other program.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, artist photos from Deezer and translations from Google Translate. None of them needs an account, and the player works offline.
